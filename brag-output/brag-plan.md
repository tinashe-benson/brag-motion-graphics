# Brag Plan: Argo

## What is this app?
Argo is "the AI Agent for scaling founders": an agent with context, governance and operational control built in. It runs on Argo FounderOS™, a private business intelligence layer that connects a founder's tools, memory, SOPs and goals, so the agent "never starts from scratch — it starts from your business."

## Source material (site blocked by network policy)
www.hireargo.ai was denied by this environment's egress proxy, so the copy below comes from search-indexed text of hireargo.ai. The product UI comes from the user's three dashboard screenshots (Connected apps, Skills). Every product claim on screen is quoted from one of those two sources.

Verbatim copy used:
- "The AI Agent for scaling founders"
- "Context, governance, and operational control built in"
- "Argo FounderOS™ — your private business intelligence layer"

- Dashboard: "Connect your own work accounts so Argo can use them for you. Only you can see or use your connections." / Yours (9) / Available (36) / 22 active skills / sidebar items.

Privacy: the screenshot shows a real email in the sidebar. The video replaces it with the fictional "maya@northwind.co". All Slack people, channel and message details are fictional, illustrative UI text.

## The angle
A founder's day is nine tabs and zero context. Argo is the one teammate that already knows the business. The video goes from noise to one agent, shows the foundation it stands on, opens the real dashboard, and then the payoff: the founder just asks in Slack and Argo does the work, but waits for the founder's approval. Governance is the closing flex, not fine print.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark navy void fills with the nine real connected-app tiles (Slack, Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Tasks, ClickUp, Fathom), each pinging with notification badges and drifting into chaos. Big type lands over them: "Nine tools." / "One founder." / "Zero context." The quiet music intro carries it, and the notification pings are the rhythm.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The nine tiles collapse into the green Argo helmet mark on the music drop (8.74s).
- FounderOS: the twelve words (tools … goals) rain down and stack into one glowing foundation slab labelled "Argo FounderOS™", then "It starts from your business."
- Dashboard: the recreated Argo sidebar; a cursor clicks "Connected apps" and nine cards turn "Connected" one by one (Yours (9)); then a quick move to Skills with "22 active skills".
- Slack (centerpiece): the founder types to @Argo; Argo's reply shows live tool chips (Fathom → Google Calendar → Gmail) lighting up, then a structured brief and a drafted follow-up waiting with "Approve & send". The cursor clicks Approve on the strongest beat → "Sent ✓". A second, faster message: "screen today's applicants" → "3 applicants screened · resumes summarized · next steps scheduled".

## Outro / punchline
"Every action runs inside" → Sandboxed execution. Policy enforcement. Privacy-aware routing. Founder oversight. Then "Research. Write. Operate. Follow up." → Argo lockup: "The AI Agent for scaling founders." · hireargo.ai

## User flow worth showing
Connect your apps (Connected apps page) → ask Argo in Slack → Argo pulls from the connected tools, drafts the work, and waits for founder approval → approved and sent.
